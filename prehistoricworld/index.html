Prehistoric World

Dinosaurs and beyond.

Meet 250 prehistoric creatures, from the first sea animals and the dinosaurs to woolly mammoths and the dodo. Games, fun facts read aloud and a sticker album for kids aged 3 to 8.

What kids will love

250 creatures

Dinosaurs, flying reptiles, sea monsters, Ice Age giants and animals lost not long ago, across 500 million years.

Say their names

Tap a creature to hear its name said slowly, one part at a time, like tie-RAN-oh-SORE-us rex.

When, where and how big

See when each creature lived on a timeline, where its fossils were found on a map, what it ate, and how big it was next to a child.

Games for two ages

Simple picture games for little ones, and trickier questions about time, fossils, food and size for big kids.

Learn English

Fun facts are read aloud, with each word lit up as it is spoken.

Sticker album

Every creature you meet becomes a sticker, and right answers in games add a shiny star.

Made for families

No ads, no tracking

No accounts and no data collection. See our privacy policy.

Works offline

Everything is inside the app, so there is no internet needed on trips.

Grown-up controls

Daily play-time limit, plus purchases and settings behind a grown-up gate.

Try before you buy

The first creatures in every group are free. One purchase unlocks them all, for good.
