How it works

Pick your apps

Choose the apps that eat your evenings. Only you see the list, and it never leaves your iPhone.

Set your hours

Pause them on a schedule, like dinner to bedtime, or after a daily time limit.

Your partner holds the key

Need 15 minutes early? Ask your partner. They get a notification and decide.

Honest by design

Fair and private

Support

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Phones Down. Or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription.

Ask for a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or in the app: Settings → Request a refund.

Remove your partner

In the app, open Settings and tap Partner. They are notified. The person holding the key can step down the same way.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete account removes your account and partner connections. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.

Apps aren't being paused?

Phones Down needs Screen Time access on an iPhone with iOS 16 or later. Check Settings → Screen Time, then open the app again.
