Phones Down — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: the apps you pause and how long you use them never leave your iPhone. We keep only what the partner feature needs to work. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps, and we don't sell your data. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

What stays on your iPhone

Phones Down uses Apple's Screen Time framework. The apps, categories and websites you choose are stored by iOS as encrypted tokens that even Phones Down cannot read as names. Your usage time, your weekly report and the number of times you tapped Close on a paused app are kept on your device only. None of this is sent to us.

What we collect, and why

You can use schedules, limits and pause mode without an account. If you sign in with Apple to add a partner, we process:

DataWhy we need it
An account identifier from Sign in with Apple, and your first name if you choose to share itTo create your account and show your name to your partner. We do not ask Apple for your email address.
Partner pairings and invite codesTo connect you with the partner who holds your key.
Unlock requests, including the optional reason you type (up to 80 characters), and your partner's decisionTo deliver the request and its answer.
A push notification tokenTo deliver unlock requests, decisions, alerts and subscription reminders.
Whether Screen Time access is on, and when the app was last openedTo tell your partner if your pauses stop working, which is a core feature you agree to by adding a partner.
Subscription status (plan, trial, renewal date), from RevenueCat and AppleTo unlock Pro features and remind you 48 hours before you are charged. We never receive your payment details.

What your partner can see

Your first name, the unlock requests you send and their reasons, and an alert if your Screen Time access is turned off or your phone hasn't checked in for a day. Your partner never sees which apps you pause or how long you use them.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions in the United States.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions on top of Apple's App Store.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Screen Time and payments.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal information for advertising.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. Unlock requests are kept so you and your partner can see their outcome. When you delete your account, it is removed from our database immediately, together with your pairings, requests and subscription record, and we revoke Phones Down's access to your Apple ID. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your Apple ID settings.
  • Remove your partner: Settings → Partner. Your partner is notified.
  • Turn off notifications or Screen Time access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • To ask for a copy of your data or for anything else, contact us below. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have further rights to access, correct or delete your information, which we honor.

Children

Phones Down is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
