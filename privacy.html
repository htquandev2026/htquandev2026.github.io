Animal World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Animal World is an educational app for children about real animals. We designed it to collect as little information as possible.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo or location.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that profile users.
  • No chat, sharing or links to websites or social media inside the app.

What stays on your device

The app stores these on the device only. They are never sent to us:

  • Sticker album progress and game stars
  • The daily play-time setting and how long the app was used today
  • Which animal packs have been unlocked, so they work offline

Deleting the app removes all of this. A grown-up can also clear the sticker album from the "For grown-ups" area.

Purchases

Animal packs are one-time purchases made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store handles payment, and we never see payment details.

The store confirms each purchase and tells the app which packs have been bought. The app does not send purchase details anywhere else, and we do not receive the buyer's name, email or any receipt.

All purchases and the "For grown-ups" area sit behind a parental gate: a multiplication question written in words, answered on a number pad.

Read-aloud voice

Fun facts are read aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The text is processed by the device's own speech engine.

Children's privacy

Animal World is made for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
